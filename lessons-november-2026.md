# Substack lessons, November 2026

## Keep this playbook current

Record new Substack lessons here whenever Rajveer teaches one. Update the relevant guidance rather than leaving a correction only in a conversation. Keep the date and scope clear. A writing lesson is not permission for unrelated outreach or account changes.

These November writing and reporting lessons replace conflicting October advice on those subjects. The older launch examples remain historical, not a reason to repeat an old campaign or its pace.

## Make Notes easy to read at a glance

- Use normal capitalization. Avoid the visual clutter of lowercase lettering, visible links and stacked paragraphs.
- Keep each Note much shorter and about one clear point. Do not turn a mini-essay into several extra posts.
- Verify factual sources before publication. Keep source references for checking without reflexively adding link-preview clutter to a short Note.
- Use a sharp hook, concrete stakes or an interesting economic question. Do not invent a claim or personal story to make it catchy.
- Avoid the generic rhetorical reversal that says something is not one thing but another. Make the argument directly.
- Do not attach the same opening-question, explanation and closing-question structure to every Note. A question should earn its place.

Source: Rajveer's November 23 request to diagnose underperformance and November 24 feedback about density, links, lowercase lettering and paragraphs. The later visual correction replaces the earlier allowance for lowercase one-liners.

## Diagnose, compare and improve

Review the actual older Notes as well as recent ones. Study successful examples and apply what is useful instead of repeating a weak format.

Keep observations separate from explanations. A repetitive format or narrow topic can be an editorial hypothesis without being a proved cause of low reach. Do not claim that a new approach worked before the results support it.

For comparisons:
- State the date range, sample size and age of the Notes.
- Report average likes with its denominator, alongside reach and conversion where available.
- Compare similar ages where possible. Older accumulated engagement is not a fair first-day comparison.
- Keep impressions, unique unconnected viewers, profile visits, replies, restacks and attributed subscriptions distinct.
- Missing fields mean unknown, not zero. Do not add unique viewers across Notes as if they were different people.
- A Note with few likes can still bring a subscription. Likes alone are not the growth score.

## Replies

Substance comes before promotion. Tie the reply to the actual idea and contribute something useful. Do not merely repeat the author's point and append a call to action.

Use a short, varied and explicit invitation where promotion fits the approved reply scope. Describe the page as fintech. A discussion question alone does not replace the requested invitation. Avoid generic praise, copied pitches and naked subscribe-for-subscribe requests.

Read the whole message before sending and check the full posted text afterward. Repair a cut-off message in place where possible; do not send a duplicate merely to fix it.

The distinct-addition test is an editorial quality check, not a claim that Rajveer personally supplied that wording.

## Friday report

Provide a Substack report every Friday covering average Note likes, week-on-week growth and other relevant reach, engagement and conversion metrics. State denominators, comparison windows and unavailable data.

Keep dashboard subscriber counts separate from public-profile counts. Keep publication views separate from Note impressions. Report hidden replies separately from normally visible replies rather than implying equal visibility.

## Personal Finance: Day By Day!

From November 25 onward, end every installment with the exact text **Follow Along!**. This applies to the Personal Finance: Day By Day! series, not unrelated Notes. It does not call for retroactive edits.

Preserve this exact series title. Each installment uses Day # followed by the next number and an actual practical tip. The bracketed Tip in the original request was a placeholder, not publication copy.

Keep the tip short, educational and grounded. Do not invent individualized financial advice or unsupported country-specific rules. Check existing installments before choosing the next number. The daily series replaces an existing Note slot rather than adding volume.
