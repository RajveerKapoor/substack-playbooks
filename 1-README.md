# Substack Launch Playbooks

Operating playbooks for promoting rkto.substack.com essays; agents run the lanes from these rules.

## Objective

Grow rkto.substack.com toward 1,000 subscribers by sending readers of other people's Notes to the essay "Is Democracy in Crisis?". DMs belong to a separate worker. Posts and Notes are the user's own lane. Title work is workshopped with him first.

## Target Selection (pick/skip)

Pick: fresh notes (under ~1h old) in finance, economics, institutions or tech; notes with 1+ replies (live thread); authors not yet replied to (one reply per author, running exclusion list).

Skip: sub4sub threads and "subscribe back" asks; self-promo and milestone notes; link-only shares; AI-use or AI-help threads; political-news or partisan notes; zero-reply notes; stale notes; nested replies; Daniel Spollar's notes and his Writer Thread (strong ally, do not pitch there); the user's own "share your work" note; Opinion AI gets substantive replies only, never a CTA; no sub-backs to organic subscribers.

Source: Home feed (scrolled 3-5 times) plus "Related notes" on a recently visited note.

## Reply Copy Formula (verbatim copy line)

One specific, opinionated comment tied to the note's actual idea (no generic flattery), then:

> I wrote "Is Democracy in Crisis?" on my Substack, read it and like it if it holds up.

Rules: Title only. No links, no https, no domain, never the word "rkto". Why: a bare domain text was silently dropped once (Money Prive, Oct 4, 5:43 PM); links likely filtered. "My Substack" plus the exact title lets people find the essay on his profile. Never personal facts (age, school, city) or AI-help mentions. No partisan framing.

## Cadence

About 1 reply per 5 minutes, one per wake at most. If nothing fits, do nothing and say nothing. ~26 verified landings on Oct 5 from 9:16 AM to 12:56 PM, roughly 7/hour at peak.

## Verification Discipline

After posting wait ~6s, fresh-load the note, look for reply text + count. If count rose but text absent, wait 15-20s, reload, expand any "N reply hidden" link before judging. Landing = text visible on fresh load. Hidden-reply = count rises but text hidden by author's reply rules (not a platform limit; logged as non-landing). Silent drop = neither visible nor hidden after recheck. Drop-counter rule: 2 consecutive silent drops = halt all outbound, delete schedule, report. A landing resets to 0. Browser faults are not limits; reload once. Every verified landing reported with its note URL.

## Stop Conditions

Captcha, challenge, "temporarily limited", "slow down", forced logout or login re-prompt: stop all outbound at once, log exact time and page text, report, no retry. None so far. Browser startup failures (5 on Oct 5) are transport faults, not Substack signals.

## Stats Reporting

Stat line every 30 min at :00 and :30, measured numbers only: subs, essay views/likes/restacks/comments, landings since last line, hidden-reply count, any limits.
